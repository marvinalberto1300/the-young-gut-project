How the Score Works

Each answer is assigned points based on how closely your lifestyle patterns align with those discussed in colorectal-cancer research. Patterns with more research evidence associated with colorectal health earn more points; patterns aligned with prevention guidance earn zero or fewer.

  • Minimum score: 0 — all answers reflect the lowest-risk patterns and all healthy habits are checked.
  • Maximum score: 16 — all answers reflect the highest-risk patterns and no healthy habits are checked.

Score tiers:

  • 0 – 3: Living healthy according to WCRF/AICR and ACS prevention guidance.
  • 4 – 7: Moderately healthy — some areas worth monitoring.
  • 8 – 11: Some risk factors present — specific habits and research evidence shown below your result.
  • 12 – 16: Multiple risk factors present — specific habits and research evidence shown below your result.
