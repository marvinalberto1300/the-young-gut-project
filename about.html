About This Learning Experience

Healthy Habits Navigator is an educational module series focused on early-onset colorectal cancer awareness. The goal is to help learners understand risk patterns, warning signs, screening guidance, and evidence-based prevention strategies.

This project is presented by The Young Gut Project and is designed for health literacy, classroom use, and public awareness efforts.

Educational Purpose

This website does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or individualized medical advice. For personal medical concerns, please consult a licensed healthcare professional.
