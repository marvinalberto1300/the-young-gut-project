Trusted Resources for Further Reading

These organizations publish evidence-based information and update their guidance as new research emerges. They are educational sources and do not substitute for individualized medical advice.

National Cancer Institute (NCI)

Comprehensive cancer information including biology, risk factors, screening, treatment, and research updates for colorectal cancer.

cancer.gov/types/colorectal

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)

Public health data on colorectal cancer incidence, screening rates, and prevention recommendations across U.S. populations.

cdc.gov/colorectal-cancer

American Cancer Society (ACS)

Patient-friendly overviews of colorectal cancer, including detailed screening guidelines, risk factors, signs and symptoms, and annual statistics reports.

cancer.org/colon-rectal-cancer

World Cancer Research Fund (WCRF)

Systematic evidence reviews on how diet, nutrition, physical activity, and body weight relate to cancer risk. Their colorectal cancer summary is among the most comprehensive available.

wcrf.org/diet-activity-and-cancer

International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC / WHO)

The WHO body responsible for classifying substances by their carcinogenicity. Their 2015 monograph on red and processed meat remains a key reference for dietary cancer risk.

iarc.who.int
